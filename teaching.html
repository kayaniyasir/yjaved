Teaching and educational leadership

Teaching people to think, build and improve.

Academic teaching, student mentorship, faculty development and accessible computer science resources.

Approach to learning

Teaching practice built on application

Students benefit from accessible explanations, low-stakes practice and progressively challenging problems.

Explain. Practise. Apply.

My teaching emphasizes clear foundations and opportunities to apply them in realistic problems. In introductory programming, that means understanding constructs before combining them in larger applications.

My educational work also includes assignment design, assessment rubrics, learning technologies, inclusive instruction and faculty development. This is a statement of approach, not a substitute for independent student-outcome evidence.
