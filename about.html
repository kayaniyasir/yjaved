About me

Academic research. Practical engineering. Educational leadership.

My work spans universities, software development organizations, research laboratories, schools and professional computing communities.

Professional biography

I am a computer science academic, researcher and technology consultant based in Riyadh, Saudi Arabia. My experience combines university teaching, technical research, industrial software development, consultancy and educational leadership. I work in AI, cybersecurity, robotics, ROS, drones and autonomous systems, secure communication, digital learning and software engineering.

Since 2005, I have been associated with CYAN Business Solutions in Lahore through software development and consultancy projects. I also contribute to research and industry-facing technical projects, from information security and telecommunications to robotics and digital transformation.

I hold a PhD in Computer Science from Universiti Malaysia Sarawak, where my doctoral research examined a trust-based lightweight security protocol for device-to-device multihop cellular communication. My earlier master's research focused on intrusion detection using machine learning techniques. Both remain central to my technical and research interests.
