Biography

Research, teaching and professional practice

An academic profile grounded in published work and practical computing experience.

A career spanning research and practice.

Dr. Yasir Javed is a computer science academic at Prince Sultan University in Riyadh. His published research includes cybersecurity, DDoS and intrusion detection, next-generation network authentication, robotics, software systems and higher education quality.

He has a PhD in Computer Science from Universiti Malaysia Sarawak (2020). His earlier postgraduate work addressed intrusion detection using machine learning.

His professional experience also includes software engineering and technical consulting associated with CYAN Business Solutions, Lahore, since 2005.

His roles span university instruction, ISO 27001 auditing, academic quality, faculty development and professional computing communities.
