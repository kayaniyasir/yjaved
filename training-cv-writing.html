01

Workshop activities

Participants work with their own career evidence rather than relying on generic templates.

  • Identify target opportunities and the skills they genuinely require
  • Separate employment responsibilities from measurable results
  • Rewrite achievement statements with scope, evidence and correct attribution
  • Build concise industry, internship or academic CV versions and review readability
02

Linked professional identity

A professional CV is strengthened when titles, publications and accomplishments agree with public professional profiles.

  • Present academic appointments and dates accurately
  • Link research through DOI, ORCID and institutional sources when relevant
  • Prepare a consistent LinkedIn introduction and selected portfolio highlights
  • Apply ATS-aware structure while prioritizing accuracy and clear reading
03

Expected exercise

A before-and-after comparison of anonymized CV material, with an evidence checklist and revision notes. No real participant details will be published without consent.

  • Initial professional summary
  • Revised evidence-led statements
  • Target-role alignment checklist
