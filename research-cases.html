Case study 01

DDoS detection in software-defined networks

Problem

Denial-of-service attacks can disrupt network operations and put pressure on systems that manage network traffic.

Research approach

The coauthored study combines Random Forest and XGBoost using 22 SDN-oriented features from 99,225 traffic records collected with Mininet, a Ryu controller and Open vSwitch.

Evidence and scope

The paper reports 99.36% test-set accuracy in its emulated SDN environment. It explicitly notes a single-switch topology and synthetic attack traffic; performance is not an independently verified result on production networks. Javed is a named coauthor; individual model-development tasks should not be assigned without a documented contribution statement.

Case study 02

Intrusion detection with eBPF

Problem

Intrusion detection requires practical analysis of network or host behaviour and integration with defensive workflows.

Research approach

The published iKern architecture combines PF_RING capture with eBPF filtering and packet-handling rules in the Linux kernel, tested against volumetric and multi-vector traffic.

Evidence and scope

The paper reports 98.84% detection accuracy in its test environment and evaluates traffic handling up to 1 Gbps. Crucially, its author-contribution statement explicitly credits Y.J. with validation. These are published experimental findings, not evidence of production deployment.

Case study 03

Lightweight trust in cellular communications

Problem

Device-to-device and multihop communications introduce authentication and trust challenges in connected networks.

Research approach

Research into a trust-based lightweight security protocol for device-to-device multihop cellular communication.

Evidence and scope

Coauthored peer-reviewed work in the Journal of Ambient Intelligence and Humanized Computing (2021). A documented research contribution aligned with Dr. Javed's doctoral research; the publisher record provides the formal technical details.

Case study 05 · Research and applied engineering

DroneMap: Internet-of-Drones management

Problem

Operating drones over the Internet requires interoperable interfaces for missions and access to cloud services beyond local ground stations.

Published method

The coauthored DroneMap Planner system provides service-oriented drone access, mission scheduling and cloud-linked communication including MAVLink and ROSLink.

Evidence and my role

The peer-reviewed article describes proof-of-concept applications and evaluation using a real drone. The paper names Yasir Javed among its coauthors, and acknowledges KACST grant 35-157. The Senior Researcher project title comes from the academic CV, not a claim of sole technical ownership.
