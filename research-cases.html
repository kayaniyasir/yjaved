Case study 01

DDoS detection in software-defined networks

Problem

Denial-of-service attacks can disrupt network operations and put pressure on systems that manage network traffic.

Research approach

A published hybrid machine-learning approach to detecting DDoS attacks in software-defined networks.

Evidence and scope

Coauthored peer-reviewed article, Scientific Reports (2026), including an accessible publisher record. A citable research contribution to data-driven network defence. Specific evaluation results should be taken from the paper, not inferred from this summary.

Case study 02

Intrusion detection with eBPF

Problem

Intrusion detection requires practical analysis of network or host behaviour and integration with defensive workflows.

Research approach

Investigation of an eBPF-oriented intrusion detection and prevention approach operating at the kernel level.

Evidence and scope

Coauthored peer-reviewed article, Technologies 12(8), 122 (2024). A published implementation-oriented security research contribution. Performance claims require direct reading of the paper.

Case study 03

Lightweight trust in cellular communications

Problem

Device-to-device and multihop communications introduce authentication and trust challenges in connected networks.

Research approach

Research into a trust-based lightweight security protocol for device-to-device multihop cellular communication.

Evidence and scope

Coauthored peer-reviewed work in the Journal of Ambient Intelligence and Humanized Computing (2021). A documented research contribution aligned with Dr. Javed's doctoral research; the publisher record provides the formal technical details.

Case study 04

Evidence-led university quality assurance

Problem

Universities need reliable information flows and KPI records to support improvement, governance and accreditation.

Research approach

Case study of data integration, KPI definitions and quality-assurance practices at Prince Sultan University.

Evidence and scope

Javed and Alenezi, Sustainability 15(10), 8136 (2023), including author contributions. A published account of practical implementation and lessons for higher education quality systems.
