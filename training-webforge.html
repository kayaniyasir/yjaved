AI-assisted software development

WebForge 2026

Learn to plan, build, debug and deploy full-stack applications with AI coding assistance — while keeping responsibility for every decision and every line of code.

Programme statusProgramme dates · 25–31 October 2026
01

Workshop sequence

WebForge 2026 combines three online hands-on training sessions with an on-campus programming competition.

  • 25 October — Planning and development workflow
  • 26 October — Full-stack development and debugging
  • 28 October — Deployment and production readiness
  • 31 October — WebForge team competition: design, build, deploy and present a working application
02

Skills developed

The activities emphasize the engineering process rather than treating generated code as automatically correct.

  • Translate a problem into a scoped application plan and acceptance criteria
  • Use AI coding assistants critically; inspect, test and revise generated code
  • Build integrated front-end, back-end and persistence workflows
  • Debug practical failures, deploy and present evidence of a functioning system
03

Programme context and responsibilities

WebForge 2026 brings together the College of Computer and Information Sciences at Prince Sultan University, the ACM Professional Chapter in Saudi Arabia and the ACM PSU Student Chapter. The programme is held under the patronage of CCIS Dean Dr. Mohammed Ali Alshara, with academic leadership from Dr. Yasir Javed and Dr. Souad Larabi-Marie-Sainte. The ACM PSU Student Chapter is represented by Chair Muhammad Yawar Hayat Saqib and Vice-Chair Shouq.

  • Online training followed by an in-person event
  • Development teams review, test and take responsibility for code produced with AI assistance
