01

Preparation pathway

The programme helps students become comfortable with competitive programming through repeated, guided practice.

  • Establish a supported programming language and fast input/output patterns
  • Practise arrays, strings, sorting, searching, recursion and complexity
  • Work through past or practice tasks, including edge cases and test interpretation
  • Run timed mock sessions, discuss tactics and review common mistakes
02

2026 rule awareness

IEEEXtreme is operated by IEEE, not by the training organiser. Contest rules are controlled by IEEE and should be checked close to the event.

  • IEEEXtreme 20.0 is scheduled for 31 October 2026
  • IEEE permits teams of 1–3 eligible IEEE student/graduate student members, subject to eligibility and proctor rules
  • Locked-workspace tasks prohibit AI; separate game-type tasks may allow it
  • Proctors supervise compliance but may not solve competition tasks for competitors
03

Training evidence

The role presented here is preparation, mentoring and student support—not ownership of IEEE's international competition.

  • CS Academy practice and official IEEE regulations are linked below
  • Participation and contest results require the relevant official records
  • Student/team awards belong to the competitors, even when mentors supported preparation
