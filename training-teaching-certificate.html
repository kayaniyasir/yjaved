Programme statusTeaching development · Practical pathway
01

Core training components

Programme design emphasizes constructive alignment and practical transfer to real teaching.

  • Write observable, assessable learning outcomes
  • Connect classroom activity and assessment to intended outcomes
  • Build transparent assessment tasks, marking rubrics and feedback cycles
  • Use inclusive teaching choices and learning technology thoughtfully
02

Practical teaching portfolio

Each participant should produce teaching artifacts rather than just listen to presentations.

  • Lesson or microteaching plan with intended learning
  • Assessment/rubric and a sample of useful feedback
  • Reflection based on learner evidence and instructor observation
  • Action plan for the next delivery of the activity
03

Certification and institutional delivery

A workshop or teaching-development series does not automatically award a teaching qualification. Certificate wording, credit, assessment and awarding institution must be defined by the responsible university or approved provider.

  • Distinguish facilitator, programme developer and credential-awarding institution
  • Use institutional requirements for any formal certificate
  • Evaluate learning through participant work, teaching observation and reflective feedback
