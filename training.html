The method behind the training

Every programme should produce something useful.

Start with a problem

A practical challenge or authentic learning need rather than a slide-only overview.

Practice with guidance

Examples, coaching and exercises that make participants think and test their work.

Demonstrate learning

Working code, a CV draft, a lesson plan or an AI-risk decision record.

Training approach: Programmes combine guided practice, peer feedback and practical work. Formal certificates, where applicable, are issued by the responsible institution.

Institutional collaboration

Training tailored to a real learning need.

Programmes can be adapted for student groups, universities and professional teams, subject to programme responsibilities and available delivery arrangements.
