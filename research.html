Research Areas & Contributions

Research themes supported by selected published work, funded projects, technical collaborations and education initiatives.

View major publicationsResearch collaboration

My research spans secure computing, next-generation networks, robotics, autonomous systems and technology-enhanced education.

01 · Secure computing

AI-Assisted Cybersecurity, Intrusion Detection & Threat Intelligence

Work on detection and mitigation of attacks, data-driven security analysis, secure software and information security governance. Areas include DDoS detection, kernel-level intrusion prevention, alert correlation, cybersecurity compliance and secure architectures.

Selected publications

  • A hybrid machine learning approach for detecting DDoS attacks in software-defined networksMahar et al., including Y. Javed · Scientific Reports (2026)
  • iKern: advanced intrusion detection and prevention at the kernel level using eBPFHadi et al., including Y. Javed · Technologies (2024)
  • Reducing false positives in intrusion detection system alerts: a novel aggregation and correlation modelHadi et al., including Y. Javed · International Conference on Digital Forensics and Cyber Crime (2024)
See cybersecurity publications ↗
02 · Connected security

5G/6G, IoT, Blockchain & Secure Communication

Security for next-generation cellular systems, device-to-device multihop communication and connected infrastructure. Research addresses trust, lightweight authentication, secure data exchange, privacy and blockchain-based protection in resource-constrained environments.

Selected publications

  • Trust-based lightweight security protocol for device-to-device multihop cellular communicationKhan, Javed, Abdullah & Zen · Journal of Ambient Intelligence and Humanized Computing (2021)
  • Blockchain-based lightweight multifactor authentication for cell-free ultra-dense 6G cellular networksKhan et al., including Y. Javed · IEEE Access (2023)
  • Privacy preservation in resource-constrained IoT devices using blockchain: a surveyIftikhar et al., including Y. Javed · Electronics (2021)
See network security publications ↗
03 · Intelligent physical systems

Robotics, ROS, UAVs & Autonomous Systems

Applied work in robotic systems, cooperative autonomy, UAV communications, path planning and the Robot Operating System (ROS). My engineering and research work includes contributions to DroneMap, myBot, iRoboApp, R-Track and related multi-robot research projects.

Selected research and projects

  • A novel UAV-assisted VANET routing protocol for post-disaster emergency communicationsFan et al., including Y. Javed · IEEE Transactions on Network Science and Engineering (2025)
  • Robot Path Planning and Cooperation: Foundations, Algorithms and ExperimentationsKoubâa et al., including Y. Javed · Springer (2018)
  • DroneMap: Cloud Robotics System for UAV SurveillanceFunded engineering/research project · 2015–2018
