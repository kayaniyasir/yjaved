About Me

About my work

My professional journey began in software engineering and expanded into research, teaching, cybersecurity, robotics, technical consultancy and academic leadership. I have worked with CYAN Business Solutions, Lahore, since 2005 and am a computer science academic at Prince Sultan University in Riyadh.

I hold a PhD in Computer Science from Universiti Malaysia Sarawak, with research on trust-based security for device-to-device multihop cellular communication. My research and development interests also include artificial intelligence, intrusion detection, secure communication, robotics, ROS, UAV systems and technology-enabled education.

Alongside industrial and academic projects, I advise doctoral, master's and bachelor's students, train university faculty and schoolteachers, and support computing competitions and professional development. I chair the ACM Professional Chapter in Saudi Arabia and serve as Deputy Chairman of the School Management Council at Pakistan International School – English Section, Riyadh.
